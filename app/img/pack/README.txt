NOVAFit default photo pack
==========================

Photos in this folder are the default pictures for the home screen photo box
and the backgrounds of the weekly and monthly reviews, as long as a user has
not added own photos in Settings → Photos for reviews.

Expected files (JPG, about 1600 px wide, landscape is fine):

  campus-plaza.jpg        campus square with the library
  campus-terrace.jpg      roof terrace with a view of the sea
  campus-glass-hall.jpg   glass hall at sunset
  gym-cardio.jpg          gym: cardio area
  gym-weights.jpg         gym: dumbbells and benches

The list and the crop for portrait screens (pos) are in app/index.html under
PHOTO_PACK. Missing files are skipped. Without any file the home screen shows
the placeholder graphics and the reviews have no photos.

Only add photos you took yourselves or have written permission to use.
Photos from the Nova SBE website are protected by copyright, and this
repository is public.
